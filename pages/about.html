Hi, I’m Austen

I love building things, and I’m just as interested in the question of what to build as how to build it. That’s what pulls me toward engineering: there are patterns and right ways to build, but no single right answer for what you create.

Physics is what connects me to the world. Almost everything around me, from the music in my headphones to accelerating my car on the way to school, comes down to a fundamental physical phenomenon, and I find real beauty in that. I hope to study mechanical engineering and astrophysics, with an eye on aerospace.

Outside the classroom, I lead my FIRST Tech Challenge robotics team, debate Lincoln-Douglas on the national circuit, do independent machine-learning research in astrophysics, and produce music. The planets in my solar system are those projects; click any of them to learn more.

I don’t let myself leave on a missed shot. I won’t finish studying on a question I got wrong, and I won’t leave the gym without getting a little closer to a PR.
