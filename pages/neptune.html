The research

I combined two of my interests, astrophysics and machine learning, in an independent research project. I used K-means clustering and measured the quality of the resulting clusters with the mean silhouette score, then analyzed the results and wrote them up in an independent paper.

How I got there

To do this work, I taught myself Python and the linear algebra behind machine learning during 11th grade, then carried out the analysis and wrote the paper in 12th grade.
