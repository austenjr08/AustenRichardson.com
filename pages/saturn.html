Why I make music

Music is how I reverse-engineer feelings. When a song like “Everlong” by Foo Fighters or “Control” by Playboi Carti hits, I want to know why: is it the chord progression, the drums, the melody, the tempo? Picking up an electric guitar and opening FL Studio let me experiment with that directly.

I’ve made melodies that are euphoric, aggressive, lonely and nostalgic. My favorites are the ones that sound “wrong”: I rarely pick a key before I start, and I’ve apparently written a song in 17/8. I’ve never taken a formal music class, and I think that’s part of the appeal: nobody is forcing me to learn the rules.
