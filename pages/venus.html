What I do

Lincoln-Douglas is one-on-one debate centered on values and philosophy. I research cases, philosophy and progressive arguments, and compete at national tournaments across the U.S.

What it taught me

Early in my freshman season I lost a round to a capitalism critique, and I came out of it questioning my entire worldview. I lost that round badly, but I liked having so many questions. Debate taught me to examine the framework behind what I believe, not just the conclusions.
