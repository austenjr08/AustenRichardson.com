Your Sky Tonight

Which planets are up tonight, where to look, and when, using the same orbital math as the solar system on the home page.

The sky

Hold the chart over your head with the top toward north: east is on the left, the edge is the horizon, and the center is straight up. Drag the slider to move through the night, and tap anything to name it.

Planets tonight

    Positions are computed in your browser from NASA JPL orbital elements and checked against JPL’s Horizons ephemeris. If you use your location, it never leaves your device. Times are in your device’s time zone.
